Privacy Policy

How Job Hunters NG handles your information.

Job Hunters NG respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the website, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Our website or service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and security.

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from newsletters using the instructions provided in the messages. You may also contact us to request access to or correction of information you have provided.

Job listings and courses may link to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
